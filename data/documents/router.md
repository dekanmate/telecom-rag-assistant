# NovaTel Router Requirements

## General Requirements

Customers may use a NovaTel-provided router or a compatible
third-party router.

The router must support the technical requirements of the
selected internet service.

## Ethernet

Gigabit Ethernet support is required for customers who want
to use the full speed of the Fiber 1000 package over a wired
connection.

Older Fast Ethernet ports may limit the connection to
approximately 100 Mbps.

## Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi performance depends on the router, client device,
distance, obstacles and radio interference.

Wi-Fi 6 is recommended for Fiber 1000 customers who want
high wireless speeds.

A device must also support Wi-Fi 6 to benefit from the
technology.

## Own Router

Customers can use their own compatible router.

The router must support PPPoE authentication and VLAN
configuration.

The customer is responsible for configuring and maintaining
their own router.

NovaTel technical support may be limited when a third-party
router is used.

## Router Replacement

Customers experiencing connection problems should first
restart the router and check the Ethernet or fiber connection.

If the problem continues, the customer should contact
NovaTel technical support.

## Wired Connection

A wired Ethernet connection is recommended when maximum
stability and low latency are required.

Gaming computers, workstations and other latency-sensitive
devices may benefit from a wired connection.
