# NovaTel Mobile Services

## Mobile Start

The Mobile Start package includes 10 GB of mobile data,
unlimited calls within the NovaTel network and 100 minutes
for calls to other domestic networks.

SMS messages are charged separately.

## Mobile Plus

The Mobile Plus package includes 30 GB of mobile data
and unlimited domestic calls and SMS messages.

The package supports both physical SIM cards and eSIM.

## Mobile Unlimited

The Mobile Unlimited package includes unlimited domestic
mobile data, calls and SMS messages.

Actual data speeds depend on network coverage,
network load and the capabilities of the customer's device.

## eSIM

NovaTel supports eSIM on compatible smartphones.

An eSIM can be activated without inserting a physical SIM card.

The customer must have a device that supports eSIM technology.

## Roaming

Domestic mobile data allowances may differ from roaming
data allowances.

Roaming availability depends on the destination country
and the roaming agreement between NovaTel and the foreign operator.

Customers should check the applicable roaming conditions
before travelling.

## Number Portability

Customers can transfer an existing mobile phone number
to NovaTel.

The number must be eligible for portability.

The customer may be required to provide identification
and information about the existing mobile subscription.
