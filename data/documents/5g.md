# NovaTel 5G Network

## 5G Availability

NovaTel 5G is available in selected areas.

Availability depends on network coverage and local
infrastructure.

A customer must be located within an area covered by the
NovaTel 5G network to use 5G connectivity.

## Compatible Devices

A compatible 5G smartphone or mobile device is required.

The device must support the 5G frequency bands used by
the NovaTel network.

Older 4G-only devices cannot connect to the NovaTel 5G network.

## 5G Speed

5G can provide higher download and upload speeds than
traditional 4G connections.

Actual performance depends on signal strength, network load,
device capabilities and local radio conditions.

The advertised maximum speed should not be interpreted as
a guaranteed speed.

## Network Load

Mobile network capacity is shared between users.

During periods of high network traffic, individual users
may experience lower speeds.

Network performance can therefore vary throughout the day.

## 5G and 4G

A compatible device may automatically switch between 5G
and 4G depending on network availability.

When 5G coverage is unavailable, the device can use the
NovaTel 4G network if supported.

## Signal Strength

Buildings, walls, geographical obstacles and distance from
a mobile base station can affect signal strength.

Moving to an area with better signal conditions may improve
connection quality.
